LIBRARY & SETLISTS

Right-click a track in VirtualDJ to download it or add it to your active setlist. Videos appear in Downloaded videos after preparation finishes. Reopen a VirtualDJ folder to refresh its contents.

Choose Songs for the music catalogue or Videos for music videos. Search again in VirtualDJ after changing this. Songs may have a still cover instead of a music video.

Applies to new searches and Last search in VirtualDJ. Reopen Last search after changing this. Video downloads automatically when loaded onto a deck and is kept for reuse. First load can take longer; if still downloading, retry when ready. Saved audio, setlists and history keep their existing type.
