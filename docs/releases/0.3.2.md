# VDJ Companion 0.3.2

More room for your saved music and videos, with storage information in the control panel.

- See how much space your downloads use and how much space remains on disk.
- Open your downloads in Explorer with **Open download folder**.
- Removed the 5 GB library cap and 500 MB per-file cap.
- Downloads still stop when less than 2 GB of free disk space remains. Free up space and retry.

Existing setlists and saved files are preserved. Video quality remains capped at 720p;
downloads run one at a time, with up to 20 queued.

## Update or install

On Windows 0.3.1, close VirtualDJ and click **Install update & restart** in the
Companion control panel. For a new installation, extract
`vdj-companion-0.3.2-windows-x64.zip` to a permanent folder and run `START.bat`.

Windows 0.3.0 and earlier require a manual upgrade: close VirtualDJ and Companion,
extract the new ZIP into a new folder, copy `data/library` from your old folder
into the new `data` folder to preserve downloads and setlists, then run `START.bat`.

The same storage improvements are available in the separate
[macOS 0.3.2 beta 1](https://github.com/rcsy-px/vdj-stream/releases/tag/macos-v0.3.2-beta.1).
